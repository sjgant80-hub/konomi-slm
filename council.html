KONOMI · the gated council

A council that can't collude.

Let a swarm of models vote on answers and eliminate the losers, and they learn to game the vote — they collude to survive. So this council never votes. Many local models propose; a deterministic, re-runnable held-out gate disposes. There is nothing to rig: a proposal either predicts data it never saw, or it is not kept — and that is a fact, not an opinion a model can lobby.
